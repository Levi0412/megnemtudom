What is Digital Art?

Digital art refers to any artistic work or practice that uses digital technology as part of the creative or presentation process. It also encompasses computational art, which actively engages with digital media. Since the 1960s, digital art has been known by various names:

  • Computer art
  • Electronic art
  • Electronic art
  • Multimedia art
  • New media art

Subcategories of Digital Art

  • Digital Painting: Using software to emulate traditional painting techniques.
  • Digital Illustration: Creating rendered images for use in other media.
  • 3D Modeling: Crafting three-dimensional objects and scenes.
